Diet Plans

Simple nutrition frameworks for fat loss, maintenance and muscle gain.

Fat Loss Plan

Prioritize lean protein, high-fiber vegetables, whole-food carbohydrates and portion control. Keep a moderate calorie deficit while maintaining strength training.

  • Protein at every meal
  • Vegetables and fruit daily
  • Water: 2–3 litres
  • Limit liquid calories

Muscle Gain Plan

Use a small calorie surplus with enough protein and carbohydrates to support progressive resistance training and recovery.

  • Protein: roughly 1.6–2.2 g/kg
  • Carbs around training
  • Healthy fats in moderation
  • Consistent meal timing

Balanced Maintenance

Build meals around protein, vegetables, quality carbohydrates and healthy fats while keeping total calories close to maintenance needs.

  • 80/20 whole-food approach
  • Regular hydration
  • Plan meals in advance
  • Track progress weekly

Sample Day

Breakfast

Oats, milk/curd, fruit and eggs or paneer.

Lunch

Rice/roti, dal, vegetables, salad and a protein source.

Dinner

Lean protein/paneer, vegetables and controlled carbohydrates.

These are general educational examples, not individualized medical nutrition advice.
